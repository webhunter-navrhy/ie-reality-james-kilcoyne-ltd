Charming 3-Bedroom Bungalow with Additional Garden Cabin

A unique opportunity to acquire a three-bedroom bungalow with a separate garden cabin, ideally located within walking distance of Kilmovee village and its excellent range of local amenities.

The main residence offers spacious accommodation with some completion works required, providing the opportunity for a new owner to put their own stamp on the property and create a comfortable family home or investment opportunity.

Main Residence:

The accommodation briefly comprises an entrance hall with tiled flooring, sitting room, kitchen/dining area, three bedrooms and bathroom with the master bedroom benefiting from an incomplete en-suite.

The sitting room is a particularly inviting space, featuring wood flooring and a feature open fireplace, creating a warm and homely focal point.

The kitchen area remains unfinished, with patio doors opening directly onto the rear garden, offering excellent potential to create a bright, modern kitchen/dining space with easy access to the outdoors.

The main bathroom is fully tiled and complete while the master bedroom includes provision for an en-suite which requires completion.

Additional Garden Cabin:

A fantastic feature of this property is the separate garden cabin which benefits from:

•Bright open-plan kitchen/dining/living area

•Fitted kitchen

•Solid fuel stove

•One bedroom

•Shower room

•Attractive wood flooring and timber-lined walls

•Numerous windows providing an abundance of natural light

•Veranda/porch extending along two sides offering additional covered outdoor space

The cabin has a welcoming feel and offers considerable flexibility for family accommodation, guest space, a home office, hobby room or other potential uses.

Outdoor Space:

The property enjoys a generous outdoor setting with a rear garden and surrounding grounds, while the cabin's veranda provides a lovely sheltered area from which to enjoy the garden and countryside surroundings.

Location:

The property is situated in Kilmovee, Co. Mayo, within easy walking distance of the village centre and its amenities.

Kilmovee offers a strong local community and a range of facilities including:

•Community Centre

•Local church

•All-weather AstroTurf facility

•Loop Walk

•Local pub

•GAA pitch

•Seasonal swimming pool

The property is also ideally positioned for access to the surrounding towns and transport links, being approximately 15 minutes drive from Ireland West Airport Knock and centrally located for Ballaghaderreen, Ballyhaunis and Charlestown.

An Opportunity with Excellent Potential:

This property offers a rare combination of a three-bedroom bungalow, substantial additional accommodation in the garden cabin and a convenient village location.

With some completion and modernisation works required to the main bungalow, the property offers an excellent opportunity for purchasers seeking a home they can finish and tailor to their own requirements.

Viewing is highly recommended to appreciate the accommodation, additional cabin and potential this property has to offer.
